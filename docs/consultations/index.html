Astrology as a service

Personal Consultations

Astrology offered as a service, with care, thought, and respect for the individual behind the horoscope.

I offer personal Vedic astrology consultations as a service, without a fee. Astrology has given me perspective at important moments in my own life, and offering that understanding to others is one way I hope to make the knowledge useful beyond my own study.

This is not a commercial astrology practice. I work full time as a technology architect, so the time I am able to devote to consultations is naturally limited. I may not always be able to take up a request immediately or respond as quickly as I would like. When I do accept a horoscope, however, I want to give it the time, attention, and thoughtful study it deserves rather than approach it hurriedly. I would rather examine a few charts with genuine care than turn something this personal into a volume based practice.

How I Approach a Reading

I do not draw conclusions from a single planet, placement, conjunction, or planetary period. Vedic astrology becomes meaningful through synthesis. I examine house lordships, planetary strength and dignity, aspects, conjunctions, nakshatras, Mahadashas and Antardashas, divisional charts, transits, and the timing relevant to the question.

I look especially for convergence: when several independent indications begin to describe the same underlying theme. A promise in the birth chart may remain dormant until the appropriate dasha or transit activates it, which is why both structure and timing matter. My aim is to understand the pattern created by the horoscope as a whole rather than attach excessive significance to an isolated placement.

Technical interpretation, however, is only part of a reading. There is a person behind every horoscope, and people often turn to astrology at moments when something deeply important in their life feels uncertain, delayed, painful, or difficult to understand. They may be waiting for something they have hoped for, struggling through a relationship or career transition, facing an unfamiliar planetary period, or simply looking for some perspective when life no longer seems to make sense. I try to remain conscious that whatever I interpret astrologically is being lived emotionally by another human being. For that reason, I approach a reading with both analytical care and empathy. Astrology should never be used to frighten someone who may already be vulnerable. Even when a chart contains challenging indications, I believe they should be explained in context, alongside the strengths and protective influences in the horoscope, the timing involved, and the ways in which the period may evolve.

What You May Write to Me About

You may request a reading about career, marriage, relationships, family, major life transitions, Mahadashas and Antardashas, Sade Sati, significant transits, or another question that requires a broader examination of your horoscope.

A specific question is usually more useful than asking for a general prediction of the future. Briefly tell me what you would like to understand and any context that is relevant to that question.
